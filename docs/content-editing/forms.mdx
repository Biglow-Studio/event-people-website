## Recommended workflow

- Check submissions on a regular cadence.
- Reply to the person in your email client — do not reply from Webflow.
- Save important leads into your CRM.

## Automation opportunity

- Route form submissions to a tool of your choosing (Mailchimp, HubSpot, Salesforce, etc.) depending on your outbound goals.
- From there, set up a validation flow that prompts signed-up users to confirm their email so your communications reach their inbox.
